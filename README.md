🎓 SkillQuest – Online Learning Platform
🚀 Learn Today | Build Tomorrow
SkillQuest is a responsive online learning platform designed to help students and professionals develop industry-relevant skills, build real-world projects, and achieve their career goals.
Our mission is to make quality education accessible, affordable, and effective for everyone.
🌟 Features
🏠 Home Section – Attractive landing page with an engaging hero section.
📖 About Us – Information about SkillQuest and its mission.
💻 Popular Courses – Explore Full Stack Development, Data Analytics, and App Development courses.
⭐ Why Choose Us – Highlights of expert instructors, hands-on projects, and certifications.
👨‍🎓 Student Testimonials – Feedback and experiences from learners.
❓ FAQ Section – Answers to frequently asked questions.
📞 Contact Us – Contact form for user inquiries.
🔗 Footer Section – Contact information and social media links.
📱 Responsive Design – Optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
